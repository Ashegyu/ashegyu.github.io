Oath of the Hunt Privacy Policy

App: Oath of the Hunt (com.oathofthehunt.game) · Developer: AsheGyu · Effective: November 23, 2026

AsheGyu, an individual developer (the "Operator"), processes the personal information of people who use the game "Oath of the Hunt" (the "Game") as described below, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws.

1. Summary

  • If you play without signing in, your game data stays on your device and is not sent to the Operator.
  • If you sign in with Google, your game data is stored on the Operator's server. Your Google email, name and photo are not stored; only an irreversible hash of the account ID that Google provides is stored.
  • The Game has no paid purchases and collects no payment information.
  • If you watch ads (Google AdMob), Google, as the ad company, processes the information it needs for advertising.

2. Information processed and purposes

On the DPS leaderboard, other players see only your nickname and DPS value (no account information). Ranking records are kept only for this week and last week; older records are deleted.

When you start a duel, your character's build at that moment (level, equipped gear, skills, runes, growth and other information used in battle) is stored on the server as a "defense record", and when another player duels, it is sent to that player's device together with your nickname to be used as the opposing character. A defense record does not include account information, currencies, unequipped items, quests or shop records. The duel leaderboard shows only nickname, score and wins/losses. Your defense record is replaced the next time you start a duel, and duel scores are kept only for this week and last week. If you never duel, no defense record is created.

If you do not sign in, the "Google sign-in", "Game use (server-stored part)" and "Abuse prevention" items above are not processed, and your game data is stored only on your device. Device data is deleted when you uninstall the app (if you made a backup file, you manage that file yourself).

3. Retention period

  • Account and game data: until you request account deletion. When you request deletion, it is destroyed without delay.
  • Server access logs: up to 30 days.
  • Where the law requires retention, information is kept for that period.

4. Provision to third parties

The Operator does not provide your personal information to third parties, except when required by law.

6. Destruction of personal information

  • When the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, information is destroyed without delay.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be restored.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You may request access to, correction of, deletion of (account deletion), or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time.
  • You can delete your account directly in the Game: Settings → "Delete account". Your account and game data (characters, progress, command and battle records, nickname, ranking records, duel records, ad reward records) are deleted from the server immediately and cannot be restored. The copy of your data on that device is deleted as well.
  • Other requests, or deletion requests when you cannot use the app, can be sent to the contact in section 10. The Operator handles them without delay and lets you know the result.
  • You can change your nickname in the Game.

8. Advertising ID and opting out of personalized ads

On your Android device, go to Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. If you delete it, you will see non-personalized ads.

9. Security measures

  • Google account IDs and session tokens are stored only as hashes, not as the original values.
  • Communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access to the server database is limited to the Operator.

10. Privacy officer and contact

  • Privacy officer: AsheGyu
  • Contact: worbswo@naver.com

If you need advice about a privacy violation, you can contact the following Korean agencies.

  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center: 118 (no area code), privacy.kisa.or.kr
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: 1833-6972, www.kopico.go.kr
  • Supreme Prosecutors' Office: 1301 (no area code), www.spo.go.kr
  • Korean National Police Agency: 182 (no area code), ecrm.police.go.kr

11. Children's information

The Game is not intended for children under 14. If we learn that personal information of a child under 14 was collected without the consent of a legal guardian, it is destroyed without delay.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, it will be announced in the Game or on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect.

Oath of the Hunt 개인정보처리방침

앱: Oath of the Hunt (com.oathofthehunt.game) · 개발자: AsheGyu · 시행일: 2026년 11월 23일

AsheGyu(개인 개발자, 이하 "운영자")은 「개인정보 보호법」 등 관련 법령을 지키며, 게임 "Oath of the Hunt"(이하 "게임")를 이용하는 분의 개인정보를 다음과 같이 처리합니다.

1. 한눈에 보기

  • 로그인하지 않고 혼자 플레이하면 게임 기록은 기기 안에만 저장되고 운영자에게 보내지 않습니다.
  • 구글 계정으로 로그인하면 게임 기록을 서버에 저장합니다. 이때 구글 계정의 이메일·이름·사진은 서버에 저장하지 않고, 구글이 준 계정 고유 번호를 되돌릴 수 없게 바꾼 값(해시)만 저장합니다.
  • 게임에는 유료 결제가 없으며 결제 정보를 받지 않습니다.
  • 광고(구글 AdMob)를 보면 광고 회사인 구글이 광고에 필요한 정보를 처리합니다.

대전을 시작하면 그때의 캐릭터 구성(레벨·착용 장비·스킬·룬·성장 등 싸움에 쓰는 정보)이 '방어 기록'으로 서버에 저장되고, 다른 이용자가 대전을 할 때 닉네임과 함께 그 이용자의 기기로 전송되어 상대 캐릭터로 쓰입니다. 방어 기록에는 계정 정보·재화·착용하지 않은 장비·임무·상점 기록이 들어가지 않습니다. 대전 순위판에는 닉네임·점수·승패만 보입니다. 방어 기록은 다음 대전을 시작할 때 새 것으로 바뀌고, 대전 점수는 이번 주와 지난주 것만 보관합니다. 대전을 하지 않으면 방어 기록은 만들어지지 않습니다.

로그인하지 않은 경우 위 표의 "구글 로그인", "게임 이용(서버 저장분)", "부정 이용 방지" 항목은 처리하지 않으며, 게임 기록은 기기에만 저장됩니다. 기기 기록은 앱을 지우면 함께 지워집니다(백업 파일을 만든 경우 그 파일은 이용자가 직접 관리합니다).
